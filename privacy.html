STORE POLICIES

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 October 2026

This policy explains how Trendora uses information supplied when you use the store or place an order.

Information we handle

This can include account details, contact details, delivery address, order items, payment status and support messages. Payment processing is provided through the method selected at checkout; we do not display or store full card details in the storefront.

Why we use it

We use order and account information to operate the store, process and deliver orders, provide order updates, prevent fraud or misuse, and respond to support requests.

Service providers

Information may be processed by service providers that operate essential store functions, such as authentication, payments, email delivery, hosting and order fulfilment. They may use the information only as needed to provide those services or meet legal obligations.

Retention and security

We retain information for as long as needed for orders, support, legal obligations and legitimate business records. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no online system can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You can review or update account information in your profile. For a question about personal information connected to an order, reply to the order confirmation email and include the order number. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

Changes

We may update this policy as the store changes. The effective date will show when the latest version took effect.
