STORE POLICIES

Returns & Refunds

Effective date: 10 October 2026

If there is a problem with an order, contact us promptly by replying to the order confirmation email and include the order number and a short description of the issue.

Before requesting a return

Keep the item, packaging and proof of purchase until the request is reviewed. Include clear photographs for damaged, incorrect or defective items where relevant. Do not send an item back until you receive return instructions.

Eligibility

Eligibility depends on the item, its condition, the reason for the request and applicable consumer law. Items that have been used, altered, damaged after delivery, or are not in their original condition may not be eligible except where required by law.

Damaged or incorrect items

Report delivery damage, missing items or an incorrect item as soon as reasonably possible after delivery. We will review the order details and may offer a replacement, return instructions, store credit or refund where appropriate.

Refund timing

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method where possible. The time for a refund to appear depends on the payment provider or bank. Delivery and service charges are handled according to the reason for the return and applicable law.

Order cancellation

Contact us immediately if you need to cancel. A cancellation cannot be guaranteed once an order has been processed or dispatched, but we will confirm the available options.
